# Style bible (DRAFT — locked after Dima picks a look in Phase 2)

The machine-readable version every prompt uses is `project/style.yaml`. Keep both in sync.

## Look
- **Past (1900s–1930s):** realistic 35mm documentary feel; natural warm light; slightly faded colour;
  subtle grain. Real textures: dust, stone, worn cloth.
- **Dramatic beats** (`past_dramatic`): golden hour or dusk, long shadows, stronger contrast.
  For turning points, chapter openings, the ending of each chapter.
- **Present-day ending:** cleaner, brighter, crisp, still natural light.

## Camera
Slow and calm: gentle push-ins, slow pans, slow glides. No fast moves, no whip pans, no shaky cam.

## People
Real women → never AI faces. People seen from behind, in silhouette, at a distance, or hands only.

## Period details
Long skirts, headscarves, simple blouses, work shirts, boots or sandals. Hoes, sickles, wooden ploughs,
mules, horses, carts. Stone and mud-brick buildings, tents, wooden huts, eucalyptus, rocky hills,
swampy valleys, the Kinneret lake.

## Never (negative list)
Modern clothing, jeans, sneakers, plastic, cars, tractors, power lines, asphalt, concrete blocks,
phones, sunglasses, zippers, text, watermarks, logos, cartoon/CGI look, distorted hands or faces.

## Format
16:9 · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · stills generated at 2K, 16:9.

## Typography
Titles in Russian, serif (Constantia), warm off-white on near-black, subtle slow push-in.
Maps: parchment land, grey-green sea, deep red markers and routes, Russian labels.
